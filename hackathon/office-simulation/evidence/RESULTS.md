## Time and cost

- Real time for the eight days on this machine: 118 s in total, 2239 API calls. Median intake pass per day: 14 to 31 ms. This is the sandbox with no model call and no provider network: it is not a latency figure for a live deployment.
- Model usage and cost: not measurable. No model call was recorded in any run (the Desk's per-run usage is null, which means unknown, not zero).

## An assumption, not a measurement

At the per-item minutes assumed in `officesim/jobmodel.py`, the work the oracle confirmed would take a human assistant about 293 minutes across the eight days, against about 24 minutes of the executive's decisions in the Desk. Those per-item minutes were written by the simulator's author. They were not measured from a job-holder and must not be quoted as time saved.
